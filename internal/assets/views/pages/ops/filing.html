{{template "icon" "folder"}} Where things live

Short version: don't worry about it.

When your AI saves something here, tmp reads it and picks a folder. It is right most of the time and wrong some of the time, and the wrong times matter far less than you think.

At some point you will open the sidebar, find a lasagne recipe filed under /business, and feel something. Let it pass. Nothing is lost. Search will find it. The recipe does not taste different.

This site is mostly read by machines

That is the bit that makes the filing unimportant. When you ask your AI about something in here, it searches the text and reads the page. It does not walk the folder tree admiring your taxonomy. It never once thinks "ah, but is this really business?"

Folders exist for one job: so that when you open the sidebar, you see nine things instead of four hundred. That is the whole job. Anything beyond that is you doing homework for an audience of nobody.

How it decides, if you are curious

There are five top-level folders and tmp will not invent a sixth:

  • /business — work: clients, deals, meetings, your own products
  • /personal — life: family, health, home, money, travel
  • /projects — a named thing you are building or running
  • /research — the outside world: other people's products, markets, papers
  • /inbox — it genuinely could not tell

Anything more specific goes one level down, named after the subject: /business/acme, /personal/travel, /research/password-managers. Three levels deep is the limit, because nobody has ever been glad of a fourth.

/inbox is not a failure. It is tmp admitting it does not know, which is better than a confident wrong answer. If things pile up there, it usually means you have a subject that deserves a folder and does not have one yet.

If a page is somewhere silly

Open it and press Find it a home. tmp looks at the page again, without seeing where it currently sits — otherwise it just agrees with itself — and moves it if it has a better idea. The old address keeps working, so nothing you have linked or shared breaks.

If it is already in a reasonable place it will say so and leave it alone.

Things you do not need to do

  • Design a folder structure before you start. It builds itself.
  • Tidy up on a Sunday. Nobody is coming to inspect it.
  • Move a document because it is almost in the right place.
  • Care about /inbox having things in it.

Write things down. Ask your AI for them later. That is the product.
